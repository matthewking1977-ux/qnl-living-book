# Pop-up artwork brief (for image generation)

Each spread needs **6 cut-out layers** (24 images in total), plus one optional flat page background per spread. Generate all six layers of a spread in the **same ChatGPT conversation** so the style stays consistent, and paste the style block first.

## Rules for every layer

- **Transparent background** PNG, about 2000 px on the long side.
- **Flat, straight-on view.** No perspective, no camera angle. The website supplies the 3D.
- **Straight bottom edge.** The artwork must run right down to the bottom of the image, because that edge is the fold line where the layer is glued to the page.
- **One cut-out per image.** A single connected paper shape, like one die-cut piece of card.
- A thin **off-white paper border** (about 6 px) around the silhouette, as on a real die-cut.
- **No drop shadow, no floor, no text, no logos, no real people's faces.**
- Save as `spreadA-layer1.png` … `spreadD-layer6.png`. Layer 1 is the back, layer 6 the front.

## Style block (paste at the start of each conversation)

> You are illustrating die-cut layers for a premium paper pop-up book for Qatar National Library. Style: layered cut-paper illustration with visible paper texture and soft hand-painted gouache colour, rich and saturated but refined, editorial rather than cartoonish. Clean shapes, confident silhouettes, small crafted details. Each image is ONE die-cut card piece seen perfectly flat and straight-on, with a thin off-white paper border around its outline, on a fully transparent background. The artwork must touch the bottom edge of the image along a straight horizontal line. No shadows, no ground plane, no text, no logos. Keep this exact style and palette for every image I ask for in this conversation.

## Layer shapes (same for every spread)

| Layer | Role | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Backdrop, stands at the back | wide, 3:2 landscape |
| 2 | Midground scene | wide, 3:2 landscape, lower than layer 1 |
| 3 | Hero, centre stage | roughly square |
| 4 | Left wing | 3:4 portrait |
| 5 | Right wing | 3:4 portrait |
| 6 | Foreground strip | long and low, 4:1 |
| 7 (optional) | Flat page background printed on the paper | 3:2 landscape, NOT transparent, quiet in the lower third where the text sits |

## Spread A · Children's Library

Palette: deep indigo night, warm yellow, coral, leaf green.

1. **Backdrop.** A tall arched night sky in deep indigo with a large glowing crescent moon, scattered stars and a few soft clouds. Arched top, straight bottom edge.
2. **Midground.** A row of rounded storybook hills with small houses, a winding path and tiny lit windows, in greens and warm yellows.
3. **Hero.** A large leafy tree with a wide canopy, a child sitting against the trunk reading an open book, with a lantern beside them. Child shown from the side or back, simple and stylised.
4. **Left wing.** A stack of oversized picture books with a friendly fox sitting on top, looking toward the right.
5. **Right wing.** A hot-air balloon made of book pages rising above a small cloud, with a rope trailing down to the bottom edge.
6. **Foreground strip.** Tall grass, wildflowers, toadstools and a few open books lying in the grass.
7. **Page background (optional).** A soft meadow at dusk seen from above, plain and calm in the lower third.
